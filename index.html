How to Play

  • Goal. Bring the enemy Ward from 20 to 0 before your own Ward falls.
  • Mana. Every turn you gain +1 max mana (up to 10) and it refills in full, and you draw 1 card. The Warden gains mana exactly the same way - every card costs mana, for both sides.
  • Playing cards. Click a card in your hand to play it. Creatures take the field (max 5); spells fire at once.
  • Attacking. Click one of your creatures and it attacks at once: a Guard first, then a creature it can destroy, otherwise the enemy Ward. A creature that just arrived waits one turn before attacking - unless it is Swift.
  • Guard. While a Guard stands on a field, enemies must attack it before anything else.
  • The clock. You have 15 seconds to summon each turn. If time runs out and you summoned nothing, the Rift burns your Ward for 2. When you have nothing left to do, your turn passes by itself - no need to press End Turn.
  • Empty deck. When your deck runs out, every card you would draw burns your Ward for 1 instead.
